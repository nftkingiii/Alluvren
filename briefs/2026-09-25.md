# Alluvren — product upgrades (2026-09-25)

## 1. Investors now get private outcomes (v0.2.0)
- When a redemption batch is finalized, each investor automatically receives two private records:
  - an **entitlement** for the units they were allocated;
  - an **outstanding record** for the units the window could not fund.
- Only that investor can see their records. Other investors, the operator and the reviewers cannot.
- Investors **acknowledge** an entitlement or **withdraw** outstanding units themselves, once each; repeats are rejected. Both produce a receipt, clearly labelled as a demo acknowledgment (no money moves).
- If finalization is rejected, no investor records are created.
- Verified on BitSafe LocalNet through a real governed finalization, not just in tests.

## 2. Approval rules are now a governed fund policy (v0.3.0)
Built around the five parties the interviews described: COO, Operations, Treasury, Compliance and the Administrator.
- **Flexible roles and quorums:** a fund policy on the ledger lists each role, who may fill it, and how many must approve (e.g. Treasury 1-of-2, COO final sign-off). New roles: Compliance, Administrator check, Final sign-off.
- **Stricter separation of duties:** an approver cannot be an investor in the batch, cannot approve under two roles, and cannot be the proposer or operator. Extra or duplicate approvals are rejected, so the approved set is exact.
- **Sign-off that scales with risk:** the ledger works out extra requirements from the batch itself, so the backend cannot skip them:
  - funded below 50% → Compliance must approve;
  - one investor receiving over 80% of the liquidity → Treasury needs 2 approvals;
  - any exception flagged on the batch → Compliance must approve.
- **Rule changes are governed:** creating or changing the policy requires the BitSafe member threshold, versions advance one at a time, and a change blocks batches approved under the old rules until they are re-frozen.
- **Existing batches keep working:** batches created before the policy use the original two-reviewer rule, now with a conflict-of-interest check added.
- Status: built and tested locally (all rule checks pass, and each safeguard was shown to be caught if removed). The BitSafe LocalNet run of the policy flow is next.
